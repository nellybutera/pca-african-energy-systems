# Data dictionary

Source: [Our World in Data energy dataset](https://github.com/owid/energy-data), file `owid-energy-data.csv` at commit `7e387a1`. The analysis keeps 54 African countries for 2000-2022 (1,230 country-years). Definitions and units follow the OWID codebook.

## Identifiers and labels

| Column | Meaning | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| `country` | Country name; retained as a label, not a PCA input. | None (text) |
| `iso_code` | Three-letter ISO country code used to select African records. | None (code) |
| `year` | Calendar year of the observation. | Year |
| `sub_region` | Added label assigning each country to Central, East, North, Southern or West Africa. It is one-hot encoded for PCA, with Central as the reference. | None (category) |

## Numeric PCA features

| Column | Meaning | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| `gdp` | Total GDP, adjusted for inflation and differences in living costs. | International-$ in 2011 prices |
| `population` | Population of the country. | People |
| `primary_energy_consumption` | Total primary energy consumption. | Terawatt-hours (TWh) |
| `energy_per_capita` | Primary energy consumption per person. | Kilowatt-hours per person (kWh) |
| `electricity_demand` | Total electricity demand. | Terawatt-hours (TWh) |
| `per_capita_electricity` | Total electricity generation per person. | Kilowatt-hours per person (kWh) |
| `fossil_share_elec` | Share of electricity generated from coal, oil and gas. | Percent (%) |
| `renewables_share_elec` | Share of electricity generated from renewable sources. | Percent (%) |
| `hydro_share_elec` | Share of electricity generated by hydropower. | Percent (%) |
| `solar_share_elec` | Share of electricity generated by solar power. | Percent (%) |
| `oil_production` | Oil production. | Terawatt-hours (TWh) |
| `gas_production` | Gas production. | Terawatt-hours (TWh) |
| `coal_production` | Coal production. | Terawatt-hours (TWh) |
| `greenhouse_gas_emissions` | Lifecycle emissions from electricity generation. | Million tonnes of CO2 equivalents (Mt CO2e) |
| `net_elec_imports_share_demand` | Net electricity imports (imports minus exports) as a share of electricity demand. | Percent (%) |

## Processing notes

- The raw file contains missing values. Oil, gas and coal production gaps are interpolated within each country's time series; GDP gaps for countries with no reported GDP use the sub-region median.
- Skewed, non-negative numeric measures are log-transformed when their measured skewness exceeds 1. The numeric columns and four one-hot sub-region columns are standardized before PCA.
- The final PCA matrix has 19 features: 15 numeric measures and four region indicators (`region_East`, `region_North`, `region_Southern`, `region_West`). Central is the reference category.
